Hybrid Athlete / Design hand-off / Redesign v1
A training notebook, not a game

Same pages, same IDs, same flows as the current app (P01 to P06). What changes is the visual system: warm graphite surfaces, bone-white type, one red signal, and numbers treated as the hero. Built for a person who trains between meetings and wants the app to feel calm, precise and premium.

One red

Red appears only on the primary action, the current week, progress fill and the active input. Everything else is graphite and bone.

Numbers lead

Week numbers, kg and reps use Saira Condensed with tabular figures, like a scoreboard. They are the most important thing on every screen.

Quiet surfaces

No gradients, glows, italics or emoji. Depth comes from three surface steps and hairlines. The only gradient is the photo fade on P01.

Progress is visible

The automatic progression is the product. Every screen shows where you are in the 8 weeks and what the app set for you.

P01
Auth entry

First screen when logged out. The coach's photo replaces the gradient text: people buy from a person.

Layout, top to bottom

  • Full-bleed photo, 500px tall, object-position: 50% 30%, filter saturate(.82) contrast(1.06) brightness(.88). Overlay fades to --bg between 34% and 86% so the headline always sits on dark, plus a light top fade for the status bar.
  • Top bar: logo mark 22px + wordmark (Label style, +0.18em). Language switch as plain text, active in --tx, others --tx3.
  • Content pinned to the bottom, 24px gutters: eyebrow with 18x2 red rule, Display XL "Hybrid athlete" (72/60, upright, no italic), lead in --tx2, pillars row separated by 1px hairlines.
  • Primary "Create account" (56px, radius 14) with arrow icon. Secondary "Log in" with 1px inset border --line2.

Changed from current

  • Removed the italic gradient headline, the lightning emoji and the red glow.
  • The lead sentence now names the product's real value: weights that adjust from what you log.
  • "Already have an account?" helper text removed. The secondary button says it.

Behavior

  • Buttons: press scale 0.98 for 120ms, primary background --red-press while pressed.
  • Photo loads with a 400ms fade from --bg. Content does not move.

P04
Train feed

Main training hub. Pick a week, open a day. All 8 weeks fit on one row, so nothing scrolls sideways.

Layout, top to bottom

  • Label "Your workouts", then the week number as a scoreboard numeral (Saira Condensed 700, 84px) with the phase name on the right.
  • Week row: 8 equal cells (grid, gap 5, 58px tall, radius 10). Current: solid --red fill, number and "Now" in white. Done: number in --tx and a check. Locked: --tx3 with a 12px lock icon.
  • Week progress: 4px bar, red fill, then "4 of 96 sets" and the percentage.
  • Day rows: 76px, radius 16, --s1. 46px number tile (--s2; the next day's tile is solid red), title in Barlow 600 17, meta in --tx2, a "Next" chip and a chevron on the right.

States

  • Completed day: tile --s3 with a check icon instead of the number, title stays --tx, meta shows "Logged".
  • Tapping a locked week shakes the cell 4px twice (200ms) and shows a toast: "Finish week 1 to unlock week 2."

Changed from current

  • "Hello, Mehdi" with the red-boxed name removed. The week number is now the headline.
  • Weeks 7 and 8 are visible (only 6 fitted before). Dumbbell emoji tiles replaced by day numbers.

P05
Workout day

Logging sets, reps and kg. The target column shows what the progression engine set for this week, so the user sees the system working.

Layout, top to bottom

  • Top bar: back button, and a 28px progress ring (3px stroke, --s3 track, red arc) with "0 of 24 sets".
  • Label "Week 1 / Day 1", Display "Upper body 1" (36px), phase chip and duration.
  • Active exercise card: red index, name in Barlow 600 18, "3 sets / 8-12 reps", 150px video thumbnail (radius 12, dimmed, bone play button, "Watch form" tag).
  • Set table, columns 30px 1fr 86px 86px: set number, target (from progression), kg and reps cells (48px, radius 10, Saira Condensed 600 24, tabular). Prefill both cells with the target in --tx3 so one tap confirms.
  • "Save sets" primary button inside the card. Other exercises collapse into 60px rows below.

States

  • Cell focus: --s1 fill, 1.5px red inset, red caret, numeric keypad (inputmode="decimal").
  • Saved row: cells move to --s3, numbers to --tx. After "Save sets" the card collapses and the next exercise opens (240ms).

Note for engineering

  • This shows one row per set, which matches per-set progression (for example 20, 20, 15 kg). If the app stores a single kg and reps value per exercise, render one row with the same styles.
  • Target values come from the local progression engine. Week 1 shows the program's starting values. The thumbnail is a placeholder for each exercise's video frame.

P06
Week unlocked

Shown when every day of a week is logged. A bottom sheet over the Train feed, instead of a centered emoji modal.

Layout, top to bottom

  • Scrim rgba(8,8,7,.72) over P04. Sheet: --s1, top radius 28, padding 24, handle 38x4.
  • Label "Week 1 complete", then the new week number at 128px (Saira Condensed 700) beside "Week / Unlocked".
  • Lead sentence, then the 8-week track: finished weeks solid red, the new week outlined in red.
  • Rules panel (--s2, radius 14): three rows with line icons. The first words are --tx, the rest --tx2.
  • Primary "Continue to week 2" closes the sheet and selects week 2 on P04.

Motion

  • Sheet slides up 320ms with cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). Then the week 1 segment fills (600ms) and the numeral counts from 01 to 02. Respect prefers-reduced-motion: no count, no slide, instant fade.
  • Light haptic on open where supported (navigator.vibrate(12)).

Changed from current

  • No tints anywhere: red is always the solid brand red, never a pink or transparent version.
  • Party emoji and bullet list removed. The rules read as plain sentences with icons. Optional add one line per lift that went up, for example "Incline bench 40 to 42.5 kg".
